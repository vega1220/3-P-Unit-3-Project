The NYT crossword

the crossword is a classic word game in which you get hints as to what word goes in the intersecting boxes

  • great for people who want to work on vocab, or who want a fun game to play with friends
  • Helps with your literary problem solving skills
  • Makes long, internet free periods pass by quickly

There are multiple different sizes of the crosswords for all levels!

Name difficulty time
Mini Easy~5x5 1-3 min
Midi medium~11x11 5-10 min
Normal difficult >15x15 10+ min

Minute cryptic

The minute cryptic is a game where you extract letters out of a sentence to find an indicated word

  • Forces you to think outside the box
  • Challenges your brain in ways you're not used to
  • Once again, really fun to play with friends!

There are three components to a minute cryptic...

Component What it does
Definition what the final word is going to mean
Fodder the word that you are extracting letters from
Indicator what tells you how you're altering the fodder

Crossplay

A 2 player game mimicking scrabble where you and your friends make words to get the most points

  • You take turns making words
  • Very fun to challence your friends
  • Also has a bot mode if you wanna play solo
